# GGS app CSV exports

`ggs_app_export_air_only.csv`, `ggs_app_export_air_soil_tent_a.csv`, `ggs_app_export_air_soil_tent_b.csv`, and `ggs_app_export_soil_only.csv` are de-identified, date-shifted real Spider Farmer GGS app exports. Timestamps were shifted by a fixed whole-day offset, stay in US Central Standard Time, and do not cross a daylight-saving change. They contain no device names, serials, account data, or tent names.
