# Grower search intelligence

Answer what growers actually need to decide, not what a generic cannabis blog would publish.

## Workflow

1. Name the grower decision (VPD, watering/dryback, EC/pH, light stress, what to log, photo consistency, stale sensors).
2. Map intent: plant/environment decision, grow-memory workflow, sensor truth, or cautious AI.
3. Search primary sources. Prefer extension/university horticulture, official hardware docs, and first-party product pages.
4. Separate `VERIFIED FACT`, `SOURCE CLAIM`, `INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, and `BLOCKED`.
5. Rank opportunities by qualified product discovery (One-Tent Loop fit), not empty traffic.
6. End with exactly one verdict:
   - `PROCEED — EVIDENCE SUPPORTS ARCHITECTURE WORK`
   - `PARTIAL — USEFUL SIGNAL, MATERIAL DATA STILL BLOCKED`
   - `HOLD — CURRENT EVIDENCE DOES NOT SUPPORT EXPANSION`

## Priority clusters

- Environment: VPD, temp/RH, PPFD/DLI, light distance, bleaching
- Root zone: watering, dryback, EC/pH, medium, pot size
- Memory: what to log, timeline, photo consistency, post-action follow-up
- Sensors: live vs manual vs CSV vs stale vs invalid; unit errors; why bad telemetry is worse than none
- AI: why one-photo diagnosis is weak; missing-information design

## Do not pursue

- Miracle fixes, overconfident photo diagnosis, aggressive autoflower recovery
- Mass AI pages, doorway pages, scraped or spun content
- Manipulative link schemes or automated outreach blasts
- Private grower logs, photos, or emails as research inputs
