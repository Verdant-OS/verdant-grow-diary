# Brief — Light-change checklist for a grow diary

- **Working title:** What to Log When You Change a Grow Light: A One-Change-at-a-Time Checklist
- **Recommended slug:** `light-change-checklist-grow-diary`
- **Primary intent:** Decision support after changing distance, dimmer level, fixture, or schedule.
- **Secondary intents:** grow-light log, plant response after lighting change, canopy observation checklist.
- **Audience:** Any grower making a reversible lighting change who wants to avoid losing context.
- **Problem:** The fixture setting is remembered, but the baseline, time of change, canopy conditions, and response are not.
- **Unique Verdant angle:** The diary is the product: capture the decision and evidence before the outcome is forgotten.

## Required outline

1. The minimum baseline before a change.
2. The change record: fixture/model, dimmer, distance, schedule, source/method of PPFD value.
3. Plant and environment context: stage, canopy position, temperature/RH, watering/feed changes.
4. Observation windows: immediate safety check, 24-hour note, three-day comparison.
5. What not to do: do not simultaneously change light, feed, watering, and environment then attribute a result.
6. Example blank checklist and Quick Log CTA.

## Evidence / claims to avoid

- This is a documentation workflow, not an agronomic prescription.
- Avoid guaranteeing that a logged change improves a plant.
- Do not collect or transmit names, photos, sensor values, or private IDs into analytics.
