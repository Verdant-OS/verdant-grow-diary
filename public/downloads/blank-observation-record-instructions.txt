Blank observation record

These are local records, not automatic Verdant imports.

Save a copy of blank-observation-record.csv or print blank-observation-record.pdf.
In the CSV, keep the header and use one row per record; duplicate the blank row
as needed. In the PDF, use one sheet per record.

Enter the date, local time and time zone as recorded. Choose your own record
identifier. Write the observation in your own words. Use photo/file reference
for a local filename or file location, and source/reference for a document or
other reference. Use follow-up note for any later note about the same record.

Leave unknown details blank. Keep your copy and any referenced files together
in your own records. Opening or editing these files does not save them in Verdant.
