# Brief — Autoflower light schedule and grow log

- **Working title:** Autoflower Light Schedules: How to Compare 18/6, 20/4, and Your Plant’s Response
- **Recommended slug:** `autoflower-light-schedule-and-grow-log`
- **Primary intent:** `light schedule for autoflowers`.
- **Secondary intents:** whether an autoflower needs a schedule change, photoperiod consistency, DLI comparison.
- **Audience:** Growers deciding whether to set, maintain, or change an autoflower light schedule.
- **Problem:** Schedule advice is commonly presented as a fixed rule despite cultivar, intensity, heat, energy, and recovery context.
- **Unique Verdant angle:** Compare a schedule change only when the grower records canopy intensity, DLI, temperature/RH, stage, and response rather than treating hours as isolated.

## Required outline

1. Answer the decision: autos do not make every schedule equivalent.
2. Distinguish photoperiod from total light dose.
3. Compare schedule options as trade-offs, not rankings.
4. Plan a controlled change and observation window.
5. What to log and what not to change concurrently.
6. Signs that call for broader context rather than more intensity.
7. FAQ + Quick Log CTA.

## Evidence / claims to avoid

- Source cultivar/photoperiod claims and distinguish mature plants from propagation studies.
- Avoid “best schedule,” yield promises, or continuous-light recommendations without narrowly scoped evidence.
- Do not tell growers to change nutrients, irrigation, or equipment from light hours alone.
