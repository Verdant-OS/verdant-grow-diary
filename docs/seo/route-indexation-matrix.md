**Coverage:** 134 of 134 manifest routes — 39 public, 42 authenticated, 29 operator, 6 internal, and 18 redirects.

## Reading the matrix

- **Static head** means Vite emits a route-local `index.html` with title, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph, Twitter, and supported JSON-LD. It is not SSR body rendering.
- **Client head** means `usePageSeo` owns the rendered-browser head. It is not sufficient by itself for first-pass/non-JS crawler policy.
- **Target noindex** is an explicit intended policy even where a P1/P2 fix remains pending.
- All protected, operator, and internal paths are excluded from the sitemap and robot-blocked by route-prefix policy. Access control remains independent of robots.
